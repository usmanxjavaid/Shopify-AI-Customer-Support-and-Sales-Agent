VELVORA SHOPIFY 
Store Policies, FAQ & Product Information 
 
About Velvora: 
Velvora is an online outdoor and winter sports retailer specializing in snowboards, ski accessories, and cold-weather 
gear. We are based in Snow City and ship to customers across the United States, with select international shipping 
available. 
1. Shipping Policy 
Processing Time 
Orders are processed within 1-2 business days of being placed. Orders placed on weekends or holidays are 
processed the next business day. 
Shipping Methods & Timeframes 
● Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days — $6.99 (free on orders over $75) 
● Expedited Shipping: 2-3 business days — $14.99 
● Overnight Shipping: 1 business day — $29.99 (order must be placed before 12pm EST) 
International Shipping 
We currently ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, and the European Union. International orders typically arrive 
within 10-15 business days. Customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes charged by their 
country. 
Order Tracking 
Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can also ask our support 
assistant for real-time order status at any time. 
Delayed or Lost Packages 
If your package hasn't arrived within the expected window, contact support with your order number. We will 
investigate with the carrier and, if the package is confirmed lost, issue a replacement or refund at no extra cost. 
2. Return & Refund Policy 
Standard Return Window 
Items may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided they are unused, in original packaging, 
and include all tags. 
Non-Returnable Items 
● Gift cards 
● Items marked "Final Sale" 
● Used or visibly worn equipment (for safety and hygiene reasons) 
Refund Processing 
Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of approval. Shipping costs 
are non-refundable unless the item arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect. 
Automatic vs. Manual Refund Review 
Refund requests are automatically approved when the order is within our standard return window and under our 
auto-approval amount threshold. Larger or older orders are reviewed manually by our support team to ensure 
fairness for both the customer and the business — this may take 1-2 business days. 
Exchanges 
We do not process direct exchanges. Please return the original item for a refund and place a new order for the 
correct item or size. 
Damaged or Defective Items 
If your item arrives damaged or defective, contact support within 7 days of delivery with a description of the issue. 
We will prioritize these cases for expedited replacement or refund. 
3. Warranty Policy 
Snowboards & Hard Goods 
All snowboards carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty against structural defects (delamination, core failure, base 
cracking under normal use). Warranty does not cover damage from misuse, impact damage, or normal wear such as 
base scratches from riding. 
Bindings & Boots 
Bindings and boots carry a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects such as buckle failure or stitching 
separation. 
Electronics & Accessories 
Covered by a 6-month warranty against defects. Battery-related issues after 6 months are not covered. 
How to File a Warranty Claim 
Contact support with your order number, a description of the issue, and photos if possible. Approved warranty 
claims result in a repair, replacement, or store credit at Velvora's discretion. 
4. Payment & Promotions 
Accepted Payment Methods 
We accept all major credit and debit cards, as well as popular digital wallets available at checkout. 
Discount Codes 
Discount codes can be applied at checkout in the designated field. Only one discount code may be used per order, 
and codes cannot be combined with other promotions unless explicitly stated. 
Price Adjustments 
If an item you purchased drops in price within 7 days of your order, we will refund the difference — contact support 
with your order number. 
Gift Cards 
Velvora gift cards never expire and can be used toward any purchase. Gift cards cannot be redeemed for cash and 
cannot be used to purchase additional gift cards. 
5. Sizing & Fit Guide 
Snowboard Sizing 
Snowboard length is generally chosen based on rider height and weight. As a general guide: the board should reach 
somewhere between your chin and nose when stood on end. Riders prioritizing park/freestyle riding often prefer a 
slightly shorter board; riders prioritizing powder or speed often prefer a slightly longer board. 
Boot Sizing 
We recommend ordering true to your regular shoe size for most boot models. If you are between sizes, we generally 
recommend sizing down for a more responsive fit, since boots pack out slightly with use. 
Outerwear Sizing 
Our jackets and pants are designed with room for mid-layers. If you plan to wear heavy base layers underneath, 
consider sizing up. 
 
6. Frequently Asked Questions 
Q: Do you offer price matching? 
A: We match prices from select major authorized retailers on identical, in-stock items. Contact support with a link to 
the competitor listing. 
Q: Can I change or cancel my order after placing it? 
A: Orders can be changed or cancelled within 1 hour of placement, before processing begins. After that, the order 
may already be in fulfillment and cannot be modified — a return can still be requested once delivered. 
Q: Do you offer student or military discounts? 
A: Yes, verified students and military members receive 10% off. Verification is handled through our checkout 
partner at checkout. 
Q: How do I know if an item is in stock? 
A: Product availability is shown in real time. Our support assistant can also check current stock for any specific item 
on request. 
Q: Do you have physical retail locations? 
A: Velvora currently operates online only, with no physical storefronts. 
Q: What should I do if I received the wrong item? 
A: Contact support with your order number and a description of what you received. We will arrange a prepaid return 
label and send the correct item right away at no additional cost.
